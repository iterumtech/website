Data Processing Addendum

Version: 1.0 Effective Date: August 3, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service or other written agreement between Iterum Technologies LLC ("Iterum," "we," "us") and the customer entity identified in that agreement ("Customer," "you") governing Customer's use of the Services (the "Agreement").

This DPA applies where Iterum processes Personal Data on Customer's behalf. In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA controls with respect to the processing of Personal Data.

1. Definitions

"Applicable Data Protection Law" means all privacy and data protection laws applicable to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA ("CCPA"), and other U.S. state comprehensive privacy laws.

"Customer Data" has the meaning given in the Agreement and includes all voice messages, message content, metadata, and End User account information submitted to or generated by the Services under Customer's account.

"End User" means an individual authorized by Customer to use the Services under Customer's account, typically an employee or contractor of Customer.

"Personal Data" means information within Customer Data that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked with an identified or identifiable natural person.

"Personal Data Breach" means a confirmed breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to Personal Data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed by Iterum.

"Process" or "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, and deletion.

"Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by Iterum to Process Personal Data on Customer's behalf.

Terms such as "Business," "Service Provider," "Sell," and "Share" have the meanings given to them in the CCPA.

2. Roles of the parties

Customer is the Business with respect to Personal Data Processed under the Agreement, and Iterum is the Service Provider, as those terms are defined under the CCPA and comparable U.S. state privacy laws.

Customer determines the purposes and means of Processing, including which End Users are granted access, what content is transmitted through the Services, and — within the configuration options Iterum makes available — how long Customer Data is retained.

Customer is responsible for:

  • Establishing a lawful basis for the Processing of Personal Data through the Services
  • Providing required notices to, and obtaining any required consents from, its End Users, including notice and consent relating to the recording and storage of voice communications and to any electronic monitoring of workplace communications
  • The accuracy, quality, and legality of Customer Data
  • Configuring the Services and managing administrator permissions in a manner consistent with its own obligations
  • Deleting Customer Data when it is no longer needed, since the Services retain communications content until an Administrator deletes it rather than expiring it automatically

3. Scope and purpose of Processing

Iterum Processes Personal Data only:

  1. To provide, maintain, and support the Services in accordance with the Agreement
  2. On documented instructions from Customer, which include the Agreement, this DPA, Customer's configuration of the Services, and support requests submitted by Customer
  3. As required by applicable law, in which case Iterum will inform Customer of the legal requirement before Processing unless legally prohibited from doing so

The subject matter, nature, purpose, duration, categories of data subjects, and categories of Personal Data are described in Annex A.

4. Restrictions on Iterum's use of Personal Data

Iterum will not:

  • Sell or Share Personal Data, as those terms are defined under the CCPA
  • Retain, use, or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than performing the Services specified in the Agreement, including for any commercial purpose of Iterum's own
  • Retain, use, or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Iterum and Customer
  • Combine Personal Data received from Customer with personal information received from other sources, except as permitted for a Service Provider under the CCPA
  • Use Customer Data, including voice message content, to train, fine-tune, or develop machine learning or artificial intelligence models

Iterum certifies that it understands these restrictions and will comply with them.

Iterum may generate and use aggregated, de-identified data derived from use of the Services to operate, analyze, and improve the Services, provided such data cannot reasonably be used to identify Customer, any End User, or any individual, and provided Iterum does not attempt to reidentify it.

5. Confidentiality and personnel

Iterum limits access to Personal Data to personnel who require access to perform the Agreement. All such personnel are bound by written confidentiality obligations that survive termination of their engagement, and receive training appropriate to their role regarding the protection of Personal Data.

6. Security measures

Iterum maintains the technical and organizational measures described in Annex B. Iterum may update these measures over time provided the updates do not materially reduce the overall level of security.

7. Subprocessors

Customer provides general authorization for Iterum to engage Subprocessors to Process Personal Data. Iterum's current Subprocessors are listed at https://www.iterumtech.com/subprocessors.

Iterum will:

  • Enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor imposing data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA
  • Remain liable to Customer for the performance of each Subprocessor's obligations
  • Provide at least 30 days' notice before engaging a new Subprocessor, through the mechanism described on the Subprocessor page

Customer may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds by providing written notice within 15 days of Iterum's notice. The parties will work in good faith to resolve the objection. If they cannot, Customer may terminate the affected Services and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

8. Assistance to Customer

8.1 Data subject requests

The Services provide Customer's administrators with functionality to access, correct, export, and delete Customer Data. Customer will use that functionality to respond to requests from End Users in the first instance.

Where Customer cannot fulfill a request through the Services, Iterum will provide reasonable assistance, at Customer's expense where the assistance requires significant engineering effort.

If Iterum receives a request directly from an End User relating to Customer Data, Iterum will not respond substantively and will instead direct the individual to Customer, notifying Customer of the request within a reasonable period.

8.2 Government and law enforcement requests

If Iterum receives a legally binding request from a government authority for Personal Data, Iterum will notify Customer before disclosure unless legally prohibited. Where prohibited, Iterum will use reasonable efforts to obtain a waiver and will disclose only the minimum information legally required.

8.3 Impact assessments

Iterum will provide reasonable assistance to Customer with data protection impact assessments and consultations with supervisory authorities, taking into account the nature of Processing and the information available to Iterum.

9. Personal Data Breach notification

Iterum will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of confirming a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer's Personal Data.

The notification will include, to the extent known: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of individuals and records affected, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed to address it, and a contact point for further information. Iterum will provide updates as further information becomes available.

Iterum's notification is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability.

10. Deletion and return of Customer Data

Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement, Iterum will:

  1. Make Customer Data available for export for 30 days following termination
  2. Delete Customer Data from active production systems within 30 days after that export window closes
  3. Purge Customer Data from encrypted backups within 7 days in the ordinary course of backup rotation. Backups are taken every 24 hours.

Iterum may retain Personal Data where required by law, in which case it will continue to protect it under this DPA and will Process it only for the purpose requiring retention.

Upon Customer's written request, Iterum will certify deletion in writing.

11. Audit

Upon Customer's written request no more than once per twelve-month period, Iterum will provide:

  • Its then-current security documentation, including any third-party audit reports or security questionnaire responses it maintains
  • Written responses to a reasonable security questionnaire relating to Iterum's Processing of Customer's Personal Data

Where Applicable Data Protection Law grants Customer an audit right that cannot be satisfied by the above, the parties will agree in advance on the scope, timing, and cost of an on-site audit, which will be conducted during business hours, subject to confidentiality obligations, and at Customer's expense.

12. Liability

Each party's liability arising out of this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability set out in the Agreement.

13. Term

This DPA takes effect on the effective date above and continues until Iterum has deleted all Personal Data in accordance with Section 10.

Annex A — Details of Processing

Subject matter of Processing

Provision of the AlloTalk push-to-talk voice communication service, and any other Iterum Services subscribed to under the Agreement.

Duration of Processing

The term of the Agreement, plus the deletion periods described in Section 10.

Nature and purpose of Processing

Recording, transmission, storage, retrieval, and playback of voice messages between End Users; delivery of notifications; authentication and access control; provision of support; maintenance of the security and availability of the Services.

Categories of data subjects

  • Customer's employees, contractors, and crew members who use the Services as End Users
  • Customer's administrators and billing contacts
  • Any individual whose personal information is voluntarily included in the content of a message transmitted through the Services

Categories of Personal Data

  • Identity and contact data: name, email address, phone number, profile photo
  • Authentication data: credentials and session identifiers managed through Iterum's identity provider
  • Communications content: voice message audio and any text or attachments transmitted through the Services
  • Communications metadata: sender, recipients, channel, timestamp, duration, delivery and playback status
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, app version, IP address, push notification token
  • Usage and diagnostic data: feature interactions, session data, crash and performance logs

Sensitive Personal Data

The Services are not designed for, and Customer must not use them to, transmit sensitive categories of Personal Data including health information, government identifiers, financial account numbers, or biometric identifiers. Because voice message content is determined entirely by End Users, Customer is responsible for instructing its End Users accordingly.

Frequency of transfer

Continuous, for the duration of the Agreement.

Annex B — Technical and Organizational Measures

Access control

  • Role-based access controls governing access to production systems
  • Multi-factor authentication required for all personnel with production access
  • Access granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed quarterly
  • Access revoked promptly upon role change or departure

Encryption

  • All data transmitted between End User devices and the Services encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Voice message audio and Customer Data encrypted at rest
  • Credentials managed by a dedicated identity provider; Iterum does not store End User passwords

Application security

  • Authentication and session management handled by a dedicated identity provider
  • Authorization checks enforced server-side on all access to Customer Data
  • Dependency vulnerability scanning in the build pipeline
  • Code review required prior to deployment to production

Infrastructure and operations

  • Production infrastructure hosted with providers maintaining recognized security certifications
  • Logical separation of Customer Data between tenants
  • Automated encrypted backups taken every 24 hours
  • Logging of administrative and production data access

Organizational measures

  • Written confidentiality obligations for all personnel with access to Personal Data
  • Security awareness training for personnel
  • Security review of Subprocessors prior to engagement
  • Documented incident response procedure with defined escalation and notification steps

Business continuity

  • Automated backups taken every 24 hours
  • Documented restoration procedure
