Subprocessors

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Iterum Technologies engages the third-party service providers below ("subprocessors") to help deliver our Services. Each is bound by contractual obligations to protect personal information and to process it only as instructed by us.

This page is versioned. Customers may subscribe to notifications of changes by emailing privacy@iterumtech.com.

Notice of Changes

We will provide at least 30 days' notice before adding a new subprocessor that processes personal information, by updating this page and notifying subscribed Customers.

Customers with a Data Processing Addendum in place may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds within the notice period, as described in that addendum.
